Our story

A melting pot on Spring Street

Pho Co. Variety brings authentic Vietnamese and Asian cooking together with the American variety-store food Maine grew up on. Under one roof you'll find big bowls of pho, banh mi and fried chicken.

Everything starts with the broth. We keep it tasty and healthy, and we make it so the whole family can find something they love.
